BOOKING POLICY
A 25% deposit is required to confirm a package.
The remaining balance is due 30 days before departure.
Bookings made within 30 days of departure require full payment.

CANCELLATION POLICY
More than 30 days before departure: 15% cancellation charge.
15 to 30 days before departure: 40% cancellation charge.
Less than 15 days before departure: 75% cancellation charge.
Some hotels, flights, and special-rate services may be non-refundable.

CHANGES
One date-change request is permitted subject to supplier availability.
Any fare difference, supplier fee, or change fee must be paid by the traveler.

TRAVEL INSURANCE
Travel insurance is strongly recommended and is not included unless a package explicitly states otherwise.

SUPPORT
General support hours: Monday-Friday, 09:00-18:00.
Emergency assistance is intended for travelers who are currently on a booked trip.
